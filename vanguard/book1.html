Three tons of black basalt. A flooded fault line. One chance to bring it home.

By Aris Menethil & Alexi Void

In 1838 the schooner Beatrice sank in a storm in the Western Mediterranean, taking a three-ton basalt sarcophagus from the Pyramid of Menkaure down with her. Nearly two centuries later, the Egyptian government hires Marcus Reed and Kira Mercer of Vanguard Recovery to bring it home. The wreck lies deep inside a flooded, unstable cave system, behind an ancient mechanism that locks the vault at the slightest shift in water pressure.

Marcus is a cynical diver and translator of dead languages who trusts instinct over data. Kira is a robotics and cybersecurity engineer who trusts nothing she has not mapped. Between them, dry as dust and twice as sharp, is MALAKH, the AI she built to keep them both breathing.
