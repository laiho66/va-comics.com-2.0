This relic was not buried to be forgotten. It was buried to wait for the sun.

By Aris Menethil & Alexi Void

Deep in the freezing karst mountains of Navarre, a medieval hiding place guards the legendary Jade Seal of Leyre, and a ruthless aristocratic house will do anything to reclaim it. Marcus Reed and Kira Mercer, backed by an AI that never lets them forget its genius, are hired to recover the seal before the autumn equinox window closes for another year.

They expect a brutal, isolated climb. Instead, they find a rival always one step ahead, a puzzle carved in bone and sunlight, and a mountain that gives nothing back. Securing the prize is only the beginning. Surviving the mountain is the real mission.
