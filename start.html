New to VA Comics? Two series, two very different moods. Here is where to begin with each of them.

VA Comics is a small independent studio. We make two things: Dead Drop, a comic series for adults, and Vanguard Chronicles, a line of illustrated adventure novels for a much wider audience. Pick the one that fits your mood, or take both.

Dead Drop

COMIC SERIESTECH-NOIR18+

Terminus City runs on soot, neon and debt. Jaxen Vance was a courier trying to pay for his father's medicine. Now he is a walking kinetic reactor bound to a living suit, and the people who run the city want him erased. Graphic violence and strong language: this one is for adults only.

Every released issue is free to read on this site, page by page, in your browser. No account, no code. Begin with Issue #1, Terminal Velocity, and read in order: it is one continuous story.

Vanguard Chronicles

ILLUSTRATED NOVELSTECH-ADVENTUREPG-13

Marcus Reed and Kira Mercer recover what history lost, from flooded caves to sunken vaults. Their tools: deep-sea technology, dead languages and MALAKH, an AI with more sarcasm than patience. Real places, real history, and a tone the whole table can share.

Begin with Book 1, The Sunken Pharaoh. It is on Amazon as eBook, paperback and hardcover, and you can also read it online in our Reading Room with a free access code.
