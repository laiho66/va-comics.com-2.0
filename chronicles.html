DEAD DROP EVOLVES INTO THE UNBURIED — A NEW ERA FOR VA COMICS

AUTHOR: VA Comics // DATE: 2026-11-17

When we first launched this series in the rain-soaked, neon-lit alleys of Terminus City, Dead Drop was the name on the cover because that’s who Jaxen Vance was on the streets: a courier turned into a kinetic weapon falling out of the dark, hitting hard, and vanishing into the smog.

Thirteen issues later, we felt it was time for the series itself to carry a 100% unique, unmistakable title that captures who Jaxen Vance truly is at his core—and reflects where VA Comics is heading next. Starting now, our flagship graphic novel series is officially titled:

THE UNBURIED
Why this title?
Because it is Jaxen Vance himself. He is the man who was supposed to die in the explosion, the ghost they tried to erase under concrete and ash—and the one who crawled back out carrying a kinetic reactor in his veins that refuses to let him stay down. While Dead Drop remains the street name Terminus City fears, THE UNBURIED is the truth of what he became: the apex predator known as Dead Drop.

The VA Comics Vision: Unfiltered, Uncompromised Storytelling
This evolution isn’t just about a name—it’s about raising the bar for everything we build at VA Comics. We aren’t here to tell safe, sanitized stories. Our mission is to push independent graphic fiction forward with raw, unfiltered narratives—exploring the darker, unspoken, and unseen corners of human survival, power, and consequence without artificial guardrails or watered-down compromises.

What Changes—and What Stays Exactly the Same:
Inside the Story (100% Untouched): Nothing in the lore, the continuity, or the tone is being altered. Inside the story, Jaxen Vance is still Dead Drop—same codename, same signature kinetic drop, same Revenant suit, and the exact same uncompromising 18+ R-rated tech-noir intensity. Only the banner on the cover is evolving.

The Full Remaster (Issues #1–13): Rather than simply changing the cover on the next chapter and moving on, we are putting Volumes 1 and 2 through a complete ground-up remaster. That means upgraded high-resolution artwork, sharpened Chiaroscuro contrast, standardized covers across the entire run, tighter dialogue, and brand-new bridge scenes to lock every thread together before the next arc begins.

How to Read & What Happens Next:
Read Free in the Viewer : Every published issue (Issues #1–13) remains 100% free to read right here on va-comics.com while the overhaul is underway.

Amazon Editions Temporarily Offline: links are paused while we update the covers, titles, and remastered interior files. They will return shortly under THE UNBURIED banner.

Issue #14 & Beyond: As soon as the remastered editions are locked and live, Issue #14 will launch directly under the new title.

Thank you to everyone who has been riding through the mud and neon of Terminus City with us from Issue #1. Same city. Same ghost. Same blood. Just a banner worthy of the man who refused to stay down, and the stories we’re about to tell next.

See you in the mud,

VA Comics
