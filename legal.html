Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

VA Comics respects the privacy of its visitors. This section explains, in plain terms, what happens with information when you browse va-comics.com.

1. Data Collection

This website is mainly a showcase. We DO NOT collect accounts, passwords, payment cards or billing addresses, and we do not run advertising trackers or behavioral profiling. All purchases are made externally on Amazon, which is solely responsible for the security of your transaction and billing details. The only places where a small amount of data is kept are the Reading Room (section 5) and the comments and Q&A (section 6).

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical data (such as your IP address and request logs) in order to deliver the pages, keep the site fast and protect it against abuse. We do not use this information to identify visitors.

3. Visitor Statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they open. It is a privacy-first service: it does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not build a profile of you. We only see totals, such as page views and countries.

4. Contact

There is no contact form or public email address on this website. Our contact channels are our official Facebook page and our X account. Any message you send there is handled under the privacy policy of that platform, and we use it only to reply to you.

5. Reading Room (Access Codes)

The Reading Room lets you read a book online with a personal access code that we send you on request. To make this work, we keep only what is strictly needed:

  • the code, its expiry date and the name or handle you gave us (for example your X username), which is also shown faintly on the pages you read, so a shared screenshot can be traced back to its code;
  • a random device number created by your browser, so one code works on at most two devices;
  • a session cookie that keeps the book open while your code is valid (see the Cookie Policy);
  • your IP address, kept for 15 minutes only and only after a wrong code, to block attempts to guess codes.

This data is stored on Cloudflare, is used only to run the Reading Room and is deleted automatically about one day after your code expires (or earlier, if we delete the code). We do not sell or share it. You can ask us on Facebook or X to delete your code at any time.

6. Comments and Q&A

When you post a comment or a question, we store the nickname you chose, your message, the page it belongs to and the time. They are shown publicly once the message is approved. We do not ask for an email address or an account and we do not store your IP address. To stop flooding, we keep an anonymous code that changes every day (a one-way scrambled value made from technical connection data); it is erased after two days. Messages are checked automatically by an AI model running on Cloudflare, and the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly anti-bot check. Messages that wait for a review or are reported by a reader, and a short daily summary of new messages, are sent by email to the site owner only, so they can be checked quickly. Refused messages are deleted after 30 days. If you want a message of yours removed, ask us on Facebook or X.

7. Browser Storage

The website keeps a few small items in your browser (local storage): a flag that remembers you confirmed the 18+ notice; in the comic reader, the issue and page where you stopped; in the Reading Room, the random device number, the chapter where you stopped and your text size; and on the comment forms, the nickname you used last, so you do not have to type it again. They contain no personal information. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

8. Third-Party Content and Links

Our Video page embeds YouTube players, served through the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. A player is loaded only after you press play; until then, only a preview image is fetched from YouTube's servers. Comic pages and some media files are delivered from Cloudflare storage. The site also links to external platforms, mainly Amazon, Facebook, X and YouTube. VA Comics does not control, and is not responsible for, the privacy practices or content of these third-party services.

9. Data Security

All pages are served over the secure HTTPS protocol (SSL encryption) to protect your browsing against unauthorized interception.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

This website is designed to work with as little tracking as possible.

1. What are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Browsers also offer similar storage (local storage) that websites can use to remember simple choices.

2. What Do We Use?

This website DOES NOT use marketing, tracking, advertising, or behavioral profiling cookies, and our visitor statistics work without cookies. VA Comics sets one cookie, and only in the Reading Room after you enter an access code: a strictly necessary session cookie that keeps the book open while your code is valid. It contains only a random session number, cannot be read by other websites and is removed when you close the book or when the code expires. Everything else is kept in your browser's local storage: the 18+ confirmation, your reading progress, and the Reading Room device number and text size.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Video page embeds YouTube players using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. The player is loaded only after you press play. YouTube may store cookies or similar data once you start playing a video; this is governed by Google's own policies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may also use strictly technical cookies for security and performance, including for the Turnstile anti-bot check on the comment forms; none of them are used for advertising or tracking.

4. Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, etc.). If you clear them, the 18+ notice will appear again, your reading progress will be forgotten and the Reading Room will ask for your code again (which counts as a new device).
