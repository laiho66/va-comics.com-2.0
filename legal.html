Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

This website is designed to work with as little tracking as possible.

1. What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. Browsers also offer similar storage (local and session storage) that websites use to remember simple choices.

2. What We Use

This website does not use marketing, advertising, tracking or profiling cookies, and our visitor statistics work without cookies. VA Comics sets one cookie, and only in the Reading Room after you enter an access code: a strictly necessary session cookie that keeps the book open while your code is valid. It contains only a random session number, cannot be read by other websites and is removed when you close the book or when the code expires. Everything else is kept in your browser's local or session storage, as listed in section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Because we only use what is strictly necessary for features you ask for, the website does not show a cookie banner.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Video page embeds YouTube players through the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, and our Soundtrack page embeds the Suno music player. Neither player is loaded until you press play. After that, YouTube or Suno may store cookies or similar data under their own policies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may also use strictly technical cookies for security and performance, including for the Turnstile anti-bot check on the comment forms and when you open a MALAKH channel. None of them are used for advertising or tracking.

4. Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can manage, block or delete cookies and browser storage at any time in your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, etc.). If you clear them, the 18+ notice will appear again, your reading progress will be forgotten and the Reading Room will ask for your code again (which counts as a new device).
