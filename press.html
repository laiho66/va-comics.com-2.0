SHORT

VA Comics is an independent studio behind the tech-noir comic series Dead Drop and the illustrated adventure novels Vanguard Chronicles.

LONG

VA Comics is a small independent studio with two series. Dead Drop is a violent, uncensored tech-noir comic for adult readers. Vanguard Chronicles is a line of illustrated tech-adventure novels set in real places, built on real history. Every project begins with a human script and a clear direction, is built with digital and AI visual tools, and is then adjusted by hand until each page carries the mood it was written for. Dead Drop is free to read online at va-comics.com, and both series are available on Amazon.

Dead Drop

In Terminus City, soot hangs over the Slums and the neon of the upper sectors is a lie sold to the world. Jaxen Vance, a courier chasing cash for his father's medicine, becomes a walking kinetic reactor bound to a living suit. As Dead Drop he walks the underground, where gangs, a smiling kingpin and a shadowy architect decide who survives.

Vanguard Chronicles

Marcus Reed and Kira Mercer do not work in museums. They work in the mud, the pressure and the crossfire. As the Vanguard, they recover what history lost, from flooded caves to sunken vaults, armed with deep-sea technology, dead languages and a sarcastic AI named MALAKH.
