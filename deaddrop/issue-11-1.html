DEAD DROP #11 - The New Order - Part 1

In Terminus City, the concrete has started to change.

The hunt for vengeance has led Jaxen out of the blood-stained alleys of the Gutter and into the sterile, terrifying heights of the Canopy, but in the penthouse offices of the elite, the rules of engagement have been rewritten by a new kind of architect. As the city begins to harden, you will discover the true meaning.

Dead Drop faces his most calculated adversary, the man who holds the keys to the city's very foundation. Experience the visceral, "rated R" action and gritty atmosphere that has become the hallmark of the VA Comics universe in this complete edition. Collecting both parts of the explosive Issue #11 storyline and pushing the Dead Drop narrative into a cold, clinical new territory, the physics of failure are finally about to be tested.
