DEAD DROP // VOLUME 1 // ISSUE #05

THE KINETIC CYCLE HITS CRITICAL MASS IN TERMINUS CITY.

Deep in the rust-choked alleys of Sector 1, Jaxen Vance is running red hot. The kinetic engine in his chest is pushing past its limits as he wages a one-man war against the city’s corruption. To the slums, he is a savior clad in obsidian and magma. To his family, he is merely a ghost—an invisible shield watching from the rainy rooftops, paying for their safety in blood and voltage.

But Jaxen isn’t the only one watching.

High above the smoke, in a sanctuary of polished glass, the Architect observes his "most successful failure." To him, Jaxen is just a data point in a grand experiment—and the variables are about to change. The heat of the kinetic furnace is rising, but something much colder is waiting to be let in.

The midpoint of the explosive sci-fi noir saga is here. The ghost is running, but the machine is evolving.

IN THIS ISSUE:

» High-Octane Action: The battle for the Rust Zone reaches a fever pitch.

» The Emotional Toll: Jaxen struggles with the weight of being a phantom in his own home.

» The Architect Revealed: A chilling look inside the mind of the series' antagonist.

» A Game-Changing Cliffhanger: The reveal of the "Zero-Space Lab" alters the scope of the entire series.
