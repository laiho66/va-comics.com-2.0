DEAD DROP // VOLUME 1 // ISSUE #01

THEY KILLED THE MAN. THEY CREATED THE WEAPON.

Witness the explosive origin of DEAD DROP—a courier betrayed by the city he served, reborn as a living battery of volatile kinetic energy.

"Gravity isn't an enemy. It's ammunition."

Welcome to Terminus, a city where life is cheap and the shadows bite back.

DEAD DROP isn't your standard superhero story. There are no capes, no secret identities, and no safety nets. This is the story of Jaxen "Jax" Vance, a blue-collar courier who gets handed a raw deal on a black-tag delivery run.

Issue #1: "Terminal Velocity" chronicles the incident, the transformation, and the first brutal night of the hunt.
