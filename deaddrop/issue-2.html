DEAD DROP // VOLUME 1 // ISSUE #02

THE CLOCK IS TICKING. THE CONTROL IS FADING.

After the brutal awakening in the sewers, Jaxen Vance is no longer just a ghost—he’s a ticking time bomb.

"Survival isn't a choice. It's a calculation."

Welcome back to Terminus, a city that doesn't just watch you bleed—it charges you for the privilege.

In Issue #2, the hunt intensifies. Jaxen is beginning to understand that being a living battery comes with a lethal saturation point. The energy screaming in his veins is a gift that demands a sacrifice, and every impact brings him closer to a threshold he might not survive.

As the shadows of the city grow longer, Jaxen must learn to master the internal pressure before the city—or the suit—consumes him entirely
