DEAD DROP // VOLUME 1 // ISSUE #08

ISSUE #8: PART III — THE IMPACT HAS SETTLED. THE FILE IS BEING REWRITTEN.

THE MYTH IS OVER. THE MANHUNT IS FINAL.

In the smoking ruins of the Stone District, the mystery of "The Ghost" finally reaches its breaking point. For Detective Silas, the Law isn't just a line—it’s the only thing keeping a neon-choked Terminus from total meltdown. But in the wake of a kinetic strike that defied the laws of physics, the old rules no longer apply.

As the sirens fade and the forensic analysis begins, Silas is forced to face a chilling reality: he isn't chasing a phantom anymore. He is tracking a delivery system. The file is being stamped, the red ink is wet, and a new name is about to echo through the rain-slicked alleys of the city.

The Ghost is being erased. The Dead Drop has arrived.

Note to Readers: This is the definitive conclusion to the three-part "Terminal Protocol" story arc. The legend has evolved, and the hunt has just become a war.
