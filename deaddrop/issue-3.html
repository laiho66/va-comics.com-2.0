DEAD DROP // VOLUME 1 // ISSUE #03

Dead Drop: Issue #3 — Part 1 of the Iron District Saga

The legend is growing. The target is moving.

In the stagnant shadows of Terminus City, Jaxen Vance is running on empty. Hunted by a city that views him as a public safety crisis, the kinetic-charged vigilante is forced to retreat to The Foundry—his high-tech sanctuary hidden deep within the sewer system.

With his technology failing and enemies closing in, Jaxen uncovers a chilling truth about the "accident" that changed his life. The data suggests a calculated conspiracy, forcing him to follow a dangerous trail into the rusted heart of the Iron District. Amidst the skeletal skyscrapers, the architect of his misery is waiting.

Issue #3 marks the beginning of a high-stakes two-part arc. The hunt starts here. The fallout continues in Issue #4.
