Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

This website is designed to work with as little tracking as possible.

1. What are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Browsers also offer similar storage (local storage) that websites can use to remember simple choices.

2. What Do We Use?

VA Comics does not set its own cookies. This website DOES NOT use marketing, tracking, advertising, or behavioral profiling cookies. The only thing stored is a single flag in your browser's local storage that remembers you confirmed the 18+ notice, so we do not ask again on every page.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Video page embeds YouTube players using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. The player is loaded only after you press play. YouTube may store cookies or similar data once you start playing a video; this is governed by Google's own policies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may also use strictly technical cookies for security and performance.

4. Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, etc.). If you clear local storage, the 18+ notice will simply appear again on your next visit.
