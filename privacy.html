Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

VA Comics respects the privacy of its visitors. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens with information when you browse va-comics.com.

1. Data Collection

This website is a static showcase. We DO NOT collect, store, or process personal data such as accounts, passwords, payment cards, or billing addresses. We do not run advertising trackers or behavioral profiling. All purchases are made externally on Amazon, which is solely responsible for the security of your transaction and billing details.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical data (such as your IP address and request logs) in order to deliver the pages, keep the site fast and protect it against abuse. We do not use this information to identify visitors.

3. Contact

There is no contact form or public email address on this website. The only contact channel is our official Facebook page. Any message you send there is handled under Facebook's own privacy policy, and we use it only to reply to you.

4. Browser Storage

To avoid asking you again on every page, the 18+ notice stores a single small flag in your browser (local storage). It contains no personal information and never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Content and Links

Our Video page embeds YouTube players, served through the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. Comic pages and some media files are delivered from Cloudflare storage. The site also links to external platforms, mainly Amazon, Facebook and YouTube. VA Comics does not control, and is not responsible for, the privacy practices or content of these third-party services.

6. Data Security

All pages are served over the secure HTTPS protocol (SSL encryption) to protect your browsing against unauthorized interception.
